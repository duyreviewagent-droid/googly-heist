HOW TO PLAY

The job: rob First Googly National Bank with your crew of four (friends or computer robbers). You have 30 minutes. The getaway van waits in the alley behind the bank, and at 30:00 it leaves. Anyone who isn't there gets left behind.

Casing: you start as a customer. Nobody cares about you until you put on your mask (hold GMASK). Masked robbers get noticed: witnesses show a ? that turns into a !, and then they call the police. The cameras feed the security room: turn them off there (or deal with the guard) to stay quiet. You can pickpocket the manager's keycard without a mask, from behind.

Crowd control: FSHOUT "GET DOWN!" makes everyone in front of you drop (guards put their hands up). Hold EUSE on a hostage or a guard to tie them up for good.

The vault: get the keycard (or pick the staff door lock), open the vault gate, bring the thermal drill from the van and put it on the round vault door. Keep it running: when it jams, hold EUSE on it. Inside: cash, gold and deposit boxes. At 20:00 the reserve time-lock opens (more gold and THE GOOGLY DIAMOND). An armored truck parks out front at 13:00.

Loot: bag it (hold EUSE), carry it to the van (gold is heavy!) or QTHROW it. The van holds 8 bags, then drives off for a minute to drop them. Pile bags behind it while it's gone.

The police come in waves once the alarm goes off: police, then SWAT, then heavy SWAT. Zap them with your stun blaster. If you go down, a teammate can help you up. If the police cuff you, you're back in 25 seconds (and lose half your pocket cash). Grab ammo and first aid at the back of the van.

On a phone or tablet: the stick on the left walks. Drag on the right to look. Hold FIRE to shoot (drag the same finger to aim). AIM zooms, USE does things (hold), then SHOUT, MASK, THROW, RELOAD, JUMP, CROUCH. Top right: 🏆 crew, 💬 chat, ☰ menu.
